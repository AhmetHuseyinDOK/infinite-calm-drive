# The real Galata zone (step 35R): sources and licences

## Map data
The zone's road, its cross-section, its buildings' outlines, the shore and the landmarks' places come from OpenStreetMap:
**© OpenStreetMap contributors**, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
(Generated by `~/cdtest/g35/scripts/gen_galata.py` into `src/real_galata.js`; the route itself: `~/cdtest/r2/routes/galata.geojson`.)
The far skyline's height all round comes from open elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM / GMTED, public domain; "Terrain Tiles: Mapzen, SRTM/NASA").

## The skyline's painted cards (`cards1_hi.webp`, `cards1_lo.webp`, `cards1.json`)
Each card was painted with Grok Imagine (anime background style) over a photo from Wikimedia Commons, so each one is an
adaptation of that photo and is released under the photo's licence (share-alike where the photo is CC BY-SA):

- **suley** (the Süleymaniye on its hill): painted from the photo "View from Galata bridge to Süleymaniye Cami" by qwesy qwesy,
  Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0). Repainted for Infinite Calm Drive.
- **yeni** (Yeni Cami over Eminönü): painted from the photo "Eminonu waterfront with Yeni Cami and Suleymaniye Mosque from Galata
  Bridge" by Tarikkaanmuslu, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0). Repainted for
  Infinite Calm Drive; this card is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **karakoy** (Karaköy and the Galata Tower): painted from the photo "Karaköy Mars 2013 03" by ArildV, Wikimedia Commons,
  CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0). Repainted for Infinite Calm Drive; this card is CC BY-SA 3.0.
- **hill** (the old city's hill behind Eminönü, towards Sirkeci and Cağaloğlu): painted from the photo "New Mosque from Galata Bridge -
  20250825" by mike, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0), with the mosque and the waterfront
  painted out (a second Grok Imagine edit of its first repaint). Repainted for Infinite Calm Drive; this card is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **saray** (Sarayburnu and Topkapı): painted from the photo "20131205 Istanbul 188" by Mark Ahsmann, Wikimedia Commons,
  CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0). Repainted for Infinite Calm Drive; this card is CC BY-SA 3.0.

Lossless master: `~/cdtest/g35/masters/cards1.png` (`~/cdtest/g35/scripts/cards.py`). Google Street View was used only to look at
(the acceptance sheets); nothing here is traced from it.
