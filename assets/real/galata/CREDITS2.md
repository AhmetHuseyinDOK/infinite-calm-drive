## Galata's painted fronts (`galfronts1a_*.webp`, `galfronts1e_*.webp`, `galfronts1.json`: its shops' glass panes listed in the index)
Each facade bay was painted with Grok Imagine (anime background style) from a crop of a photo on Wikimedia Commons. The crop was
rectified and repainted as a flat elevation of one bay. Each bay is an adaptation of its photo and is released under that photo's
licence: share-alike where the photo is CC BY-SA. The bays were repainted for Infinite Calm Drive.

The buildings' outlines and places come from OpenStreetMap (**© OpenStreetMap contributors**, ODbL). The landmarks were identified
through Wikidata (CC0).

## The second set of skyline cards (`cards2_hi.webp`, `cards2_lo.webp`, `cards2.json`)
Each card was painted with Grok Imagine (anime background style) over a photo from Wikimedia Commons. A second Grok edit turned its
sky, its distant sea or hills, and its water into flat magenta, keyed out. Each card is an adaptation of its photo and is released under
that photo's licence (share-alike where CC BY-SA). The cards were repainted for Infinite Calm Drive.

- **20131205 Istanbul 270.jpg** by Mark Ahsmann, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20131205_Istanbul_270.jpg: cards2 ayasofya and sultanahmet.
- **Eminönü iskele - panoramio.jpg** by Aziz Akbiyik, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emin%C3%B6n%C3%BC_iskele_-_panoramio.jpg: cards2 beyazit.
- **Golden Horn Metro Bridge 8458.jpg** by Dosseman, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_Horn_Metro_Bridge_8458.jpg: cards2 halic (the Golden Horn Metro Bridge and the Atatürk Bridge).
- **Kız Kulesi, Mädchenturm - panoramio (1).jpg** by qwesy qwesy, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K%C4%B1z_Kulesi,_M%C3%A4dchenturm_-_panoramio_(1).jpg: cards2 kizkulesi.

The cards' places are OpenStreetMap's (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Their ground heights come from open elevation: AWS Terrain
Tiles (SRTM / GMTED, public domain; "Terrain Tiles: Mapzen, SRTM/NASA").

Lossless masters are in `~/cdtest/g36/masters/` (`scripts/packfronts2.py`, `scripts/cards2.py`). Google Street View was used only to
look at; nothing here is traced from it.
