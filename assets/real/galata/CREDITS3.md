# Eminönü's square (`eminonu1a_*.webp`, `eminonu1e_*.webp`, `eminonu1.json`)
Each painting was made with Grok Imagine (anime background style) from a crop of a photo on Wikimedia Commons, rectified and repainted as a flat
elevation (the bus shelter's poster repainted as a poster). Each is an adaptation of its photo and is released under that photo's licence:
share-alike where the photo is CC BY-SA. The bus stop's name board and its İETT sign were drawn for the game.

The places come from OpenStreetMap (**© OpenStreetMap contributors**, ODbL): the Hünkâr Kasrı (way 939052267), its ramp (way 528081263), the tomb
garden's wall (way 1272081057), the Nimet Abla kiosk (node 14230444948) and the Eminönü İskele bus stops (nodes 11514094866, 11245684759).
