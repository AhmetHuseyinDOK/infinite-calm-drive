# The Galata zone's far city and squares (GALATA-3)

- `mass1.json`, `mass1_hi.bin`, `mass1_lo.bin`: the buildings over both shores of the Golden Horn's mouth (their outlines, storeys where
  tagged, mosques and minarets), the parks' and gardens' outlines for the trees: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
  Open Database License (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Derived in `~/cdtest/g38/scripts/gen_mass.py`.
- The ground under them: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen terrarium encoding; SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey), via
  https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/.
- `src/real_galata_sq.js` (Karaköy's square and Eminönü's other roads, crossings and fences) and the regenerated `src/real_galata.js`: map
  data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
